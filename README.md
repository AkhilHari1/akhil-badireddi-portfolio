## 👋 About Me

I'm a Power Platform Consultant and Enterprise Developer focused on low-code delivery at enterprise scale. I specialize in Power Apps (Canvas & Model-Driven), Power Automate, Power Pages, Dataverse, Copilot Studio, and custom PCF components built with React JS and TypeScript.

Currently delivering enterprise-grade solutions at **Ryan**, supporting 800+ users across the US and India with standardized UI, correlation ID logging, and automated compliance workflows. Previously digitized legacy Excel processes at **Vizag Steel Plant (NVAS)** using SharePoint Online and Power Automate.

Outside of work, I produce documentary-style digital infotainment content and experiment with local CLI tooling and Expo/React Native prototypes.
